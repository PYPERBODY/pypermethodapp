Overview

These SMS Terms describe how Thornebrook Ventures, LLC, doing business through its PYPER brand, may send text messages to you in connection with the PYPER website, waitlist, services, membership, telehealth-related tools, prescription body care offerings, GLP-1 and weight management services, account support, care coordination, and related communications.

In these SMS Terms, “PYPER,” “PYPER Body,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Thornebrook Ventures, LLC and the PYPER brand. “You” and “your” refer to the person who provides a mobile phone number, opts in to receive text messages, or otherwise communicates with PYPER by text message.

PYPER’s website is located at www.pyperbody.com. These SMS Terms are incorporated into our Terms of Service and should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

Quick reference

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not required as a condition of purchasing goods or services from PYPER. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

1

Consent to Receive Text Messages

By providing your mobile phone number to PYPER and opting in to receive text messages, you agree to receive recurring SMS/text messages from or on behalf of PYPER.

Text messages may be sent using an automatic telephone dialing system, automated technology, or other messaging tools.

Your consent to receive marketing text messages is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services from PYPER.

2

Types of Text Messages You May Receive

Depending on how you interact with PYPER, text messages may include:

  • Waitlist updates
  • Launch updates
  • Account notifications
  • Appointment or intake reminders
  • Membership updates
  • Service-related messages
  • Care coordination messages
  • Pharmacy or shipping-related updates, where applicable
  • Prescription body care reminders, where applicable
  • GLP-1 or weight management program reminders, where applicable
  • Billing or payment reminders
  • Customer support messages
  • Educational content
  • Promotional or marketing messages, where you have opted in to receive them

PYPER may send both transactional/service-related messages and promotional/marketing messages, depending on your consent and the nature of your relationship with PYPER.

3

Message Frequency

Message frequency may vary.

The number of messages you receive may depend on your waitlist status, account activity, membership status, service enrollment, treatment pathway, communication preferences, care coordination needs, or promotional preferences.

4

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

You are responsible for any charges imposed by your mobile carrier, including message, data, or other charges related to text messages sent to or received from PYPER.

5

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving text messages from PYPER at any time by replying STOP to a text message from PYPER.

After you reply STOP, we may send one final text message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You may also contact us at care@pyperbody.com.

Please note that opting out of promotional text messages may not opt you out of all service-related, transactional, or legally required communications where permitted by law. However, we will honor opt-out requests as required by applicable law.

6

Help

For help, you may reply HELP to a text message from PYPER. You may also contact us at care@pyperbody.com.

7

Supported Carriers

Text message delivery depends on your mobile carrier and network availability.

PYPER, our service providers, and mobile carriers are not responsible for delayed, blocked, filtered, or undelivered messages. Not all mobile devices or carriers may support all message types.

8

Your Mobile Number

You represent that you are the account holder or authorized user of the mobile phone number you provide to PYPER.

If your mobile phone number changes or is reassigned, you agree to promptly update your information or notify us at care@pyperbody.com. You agree not to provide a phone number that belongs to another person without authorization.

9

Privacy

We respect your privacy.

PYPER does not sell, share, or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information collected through text messaging may be used and disclosed as described in our Privacy Policy.

10

Health Information and Text Messages

PYPER may send text messages related to telehealth, prescription body care, GLP-1 or weight management services, membership, care coordination, pharmacy coordination, or other health-related services.

Text messages may not be encrypted. This means they may be viewed by anyone with access to your mobile device, phone number, wireless account, or message history. By opting in to receive text messages, you understand and agree that text messaging may not be a secure method of communication for sensitive health information.

Health & safety

Do not use text messages for medical emergencies, urgent symptoms, mental health crises, or time-sensitive medical needs. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, call or text 988 or seek emergency care immediately.

11

No Medical Advice by SMS

Unless a licensed health care provider specifically communicates with you through an approved care channel, text messages from PYPER are generally for administrative, educational, membership, service, or care coordination purposes.

Text messages from PYPER do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, or direct communication with your health care provider.

12

Your continued participation in PYPER’s text messaging program after changes are posted means you accept the updated SMS Terms.
