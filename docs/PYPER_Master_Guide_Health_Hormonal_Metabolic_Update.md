# PYPER Method Interactive Guide
## Master Update: Health, Hormonal & Metabolic Context

**Company:** Pyper Health, Inc.  
**Status:** Required master-guide update  
**Applies to:** PYPER Method guide content, onboarding, member profile, trackers, progress context, provider export, privacy controls, and secure data model.

---

## 1. Purpose

Add a complete **Health, Hormonal & Metabolic Context** layer to the PYPER Method Interactive Guide.

This update helps members record health, hormonal, reproductive, endocrine, metabolic, medication, and body-composition context that may affect weight patterns, skin, appetite, strength, energy, sleep, mood, recovery, and long-term maintenance.

The section must work for all members. It must not assume:

- every member is a woman;
- every woman menstruates;
- every man has testes or a prostate;
- anatomy can be inferred from gender identity;
- hormonal health only means periods, PCOS, perimenopause, or menopause;
- symptoms have one obvious cause.

The guide may help members record information and prepare for conversations with a licensed healthcare provider. It must not diagnose conditions, interpret labs, recommend hormone treatment, change medication, or attribute an outcome to a condition without clinician review.

## 3. Member-Facing Introduction

Use this language:

## Your Health Context

Your body does not change in isolation.

Hormones, medical conditions, medications, sleep, stress, reproductive history, previous treatment, and changes in muscle or body composition may all shape how you feel and how you experience progress.

This private section helps you record that context and prepare for more informed conversations with your healthcare provider.

You may skip any question. Your answers do not create a diagnosis and are not used to make automatic treatment recommendations.

The design must remain calm, private, and clinical.

Use:

- conditional modules
- progressive disclosure
- accordions
- plain-language explanations
- visible Skip and Prefer not to answer controls
- Save and continue later
- private-data indicators
- clear edit and delete controls
- mobile-friendly input fields
- accessible labels and error messages

Avoid:

- pink “women’s health” styling
- blue “men’s health” styling
- gender stereotypes
- shame-based BMI graphics
- alarming diagnostic colors
- body icons that imply a single anatomy
- long, invasive forms shown all at once

## 22. Safety and Scope Language

Display:

> The PYPER Method helps you record patterns and prepare for conversations with your healthcare provider. Symptoms and body changes can have many possible causes. This guide does not diagnose hormonal, reproductive, metabolic, prostate, testicular, thyroid, fertility, or other medical conditions.

Display:

> Do not start, stop, restart, or change the dose of a prescription medication or hormone treatment based on information in this guide. Contact your licensed healthcare provider.

Red-flag logic must be clinically and legally reviewed before production release.

At minimum, the safety system must address:

- severe or persistent abdominal pain
- repeated vomiting
- inability to hydrate
- fainting
- chest pain
- allergic reaction
- suicidal thoughts or immediate mental-health danger
- sudden or severe testicular pain
- inability to urinate
- blood in urine
- severe pelvic pain
- severe or rapidly worsening symptoms

The system must not rely on the guide as an emergency service.

## 23. Acceptance Criteria

This update is complete only when:

1. Health, Hormonal & Metabolic Context appears as a Method chapter.
2. BMI is calculated from recorded height and weight and includes neutral screening language.
3. Members choose relevant health modules rather than being forced into a binary gender form.
4. PCOS, perimenopause, menopause, menstrual health, pregnancy, and postpartum are covered.
5. Testosterone, androgen, testicular, prostate, urinary, and male reproductive health are equally covered.
6. Gender-affirming hormone and surgical context is supported without assumptions.
7. Thyroid, endocrine, and metabolic conditions are included.
8. Non-GLP-1 medication and hormone treatment history can be recorded.
9. Symptoms can be tracked over time without diagnostic interpretation.
10. Members can control what is shown on the dashboard and included in provider exports.
11. Sensitive information is excluded from partners, affiliates, community, and marketing systems.
12. Every question can be skipped, edited, or deleted.
13. No automatic diagnosis or medication recommendation is generated.
14. Red-flag responses display appropriate escalation language.
15. The interface works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
16. Data objects are ready for authenticated secure storage and row-level security.
