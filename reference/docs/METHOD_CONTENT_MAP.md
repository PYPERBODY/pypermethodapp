## Phase 2 UX conversion

- Fixed PDF pages are replaced with responsive chapters and expandable sections.
- Guide tables include mobile card labels and collapse into readable cards on narrow screens.
- Every chapter includes save, provider-question, and completion placeholders.
- Every chapter displays related tracker and reminder links.
- The persistent Safety tab remains available outside the Method library.
- Internal design notes and patient-inappropriate swatch pages are not included.
