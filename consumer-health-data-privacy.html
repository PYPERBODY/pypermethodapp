Overview

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes how Thornebrook Ventures, LLC, doing business through its PYPER brand, collects, uses, discloses, and protects consumer health data in connection with the PYPER website, waitlist, intake forms, telehealth-related tools, prescription body care offerings, GLP-1 and weight management services, membership features, communications, and related digital experiences.

In this policy, “PYPER,” “PYPER Body,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Thornebrook Ventures, LLC and the PYPER brand. “You” and “your” refer to the person using our website, services, forms, communications, or other features. PYPER’s website is located at www.pyperbody.com.

This policy applies to consumer health data that may be covered by certain state consumer health data laws, including laws in Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, and other states that may apply. It is separate from our general Privacy Policy. You should also review our Terms of Service and SMS Terms. To exercise a consumer health data privacy right, email privacy@pyperbody.com.

What Is Consumer Health Data?

“Consumer health data” generally means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer and that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably indicate a consumer’s past, present, or future physical or mental health status, health condition, diagnosis, treatment, care, medication, body function, health-related goal, or health-related service.

Consumer health data may include information you provide directly to PYPER, information generated through your use of our services, or information received from service providers, health care providers, pharmacies, laboratories, or other partners.

Some health information may also be protected health information under HIPAA or other health privacy laws. If HIPAA applies to information held by a licensed health care provider, professional entity, pharmacy, laboratory, or other covered entity, that information may also be governed by that entity’s Notice of Privacy Practices. This policy does not replace any Notice of Privacy Practices that may be provided by a licensed health care provider, professional entity, pharmacy, laboratory, or other covered entity.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise a consumer health data privacy right, please email privacy@pyperbody.com. Please include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address associated with your PYPER account or request
  • Your phone number, if relevant to your request
  • The state where you reside
  • A clear description of your request

Before completing your request, we may need to verify your identity. We will not ask for your account password as part of verification. We may deny or limit a request where permitted by law, including where we cannot verify your identity, where information must be retained to comply with law, where information is needed to complete a transaction or provide a service you requested, where retention is required for medical, pharmacy, legal, security, fraud prevention, or business recordkeeping purposes, or where another legal exception applies.

Appeals

If we deny your consumer health data request and applicable law gives you the right to appeal, you may appeal by emailing privacy@pyperbody.com. Please include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address associated with your request
  • A copy or summary of the decision you are appealing
  • A clear explanation of why you believe the decision should be reconsidered

We will review and respond to appeals as required by applicable law.

Consent and Withdrawal of Consent
